Your Inquiries Answered

We undertake complete residential interior design, luxury apartment fit-outs, duplex & villa renovations, and full-home interior makeovers. From conceptual planning to turnkey execution, we manage every design detail.

Yes. Every project includes tailored modular kitchens, customized wardrobe systems, vanity units, and bespoke furniture engineered site-specifically to maximize storage and visual harmony.

Typical interior timelines range from 45 to 90 days for modular woodwork, false ceilings, and finishes, extending to 3 to 5 months for comprehensive luxury villa interior transformations.

100% Vastu compliance is integrated seamlessly from day one—optimizing kitchen placement (Agni), master bed orientation, prayer spaces, and natural lighting without compromising contemporary minimalist aesthetics.

We source premium materials directly from trusted partners—including imported Italian marbles, seasoned natural veneers, German hardware (Blum/Hettich), architectural lighting, and bespoke fabrics.
