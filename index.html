The Vision & Practice

Crafting Enduring Telangana Sanctuaries Rooted in Indian Craft and Sacred Harmony

Under the architectural direction of Manav Goud Kuncha, SUSRI CONSTRUCTIONS & INTERIORS creates refined living environments across Hyderabad and Telangana where contemporary minimalism balances the timeless weight of Indian craftsmanship.

Every commission is conceived from the inside out — integrating native Central Indian Teakwood, Kota stone slabs, and honed Rajasthani travertine, strictly aligned with 100% Vedic Vastu Shastra to optimize solar orientation, cross-ventilation, and spiritual equilibrium.

Your Inquiries Answered
